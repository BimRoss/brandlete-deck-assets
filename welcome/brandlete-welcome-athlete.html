Tapping the button opens Brandlete. Saving the icon takes one more step.

Follow the steps for the device you’re on:

iPhone / iPad
Open in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap “Add to Home Screen.”

Android
Open in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then tap “Add to Home screen.”

Desktop (Chrome or Edge)
Open the browser menu (⋮) at the top-right and choose “Install Brandlete.”
Or click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a small monitor with a down arrow).
No install option? Just bookmark the page and you’re set.
